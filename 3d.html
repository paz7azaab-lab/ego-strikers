3D roster edition
Exactly 23 named characters: 11 blue, 11 red and Blaze as reserve. Play a match, shoot, defend your own goal and score against the opponent.

This build uses procedural low-poly 3D characters and a Three.js renderer. It is a playable foundation, not final anime-quality scanned/modelled characters.
